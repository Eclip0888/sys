How it works

  1. Type any question. Google Gemini answers it.
  2. Below the answer, one multiple-choice question about it appears. Read the answer, then tap your choice.
  3. The evaluation appears immediately: Understood, Partly understood or Not understood, with the reason.

Your prompts and answers are stored under a code (for example U0012) for this thesis study. Do not type personal or sensitive information.
